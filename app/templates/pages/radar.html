📖 The six dimensions explained

Power Distance

How much less powerful members of a society accept and expect unequal power distribution. High PD = respect for hierarchy; low PD = egalitarian approach. (Hofstede)

Individualism

Preference for a loosely-knit social framework (individualism) vs. tight group loyalty (collectivism). Affects how students work in teams and seek help. (Hofstede)

Communication context

High-context cultures convey meaning through tone, body language, and what is not said. Low-context cultures prefer explicit, direct words. (Hall)

Uncertainty avoidance

How uncomfortable members feel with ambiguity. High UA = detailed rules and plans; low UA = flexible, tolerant of improvisation. (Hofstede)

Time orientation

Monochronic cultures (Germany, Japan) treat time linearly — one task at a time, punctuality is sacred. Polychronic cultures (Italy, KZ) are more fluid. (Hall)

Expressiveness

How visibly emotions are displayed. Italian conversation is animated; Japanese reserve shows respect. Misreading expressiveness causes misunderstandings. (Meyer)
