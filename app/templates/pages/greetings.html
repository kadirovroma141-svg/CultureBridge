🌐 Why greetings matter

First impressions are culturally coded. In Kazakhstan a firm handshake with elders is expected; in Japan, a bow replaces physical contact. Getting the greeting right signals respect and opens doors.

🤝

Handshake cultures

Germany, Canada, USA, Hungary — firm, brief, eye contact.

💋

Kiss/cheek cultures

Italy, France, Spain — one or two cheek kisses, depending on region.

🙇

Bow cultures

Japan, Korea — depth and duration of bow convey status and respect.

🙏

Gesture cultures

Thailand (wai), India (namaste) — palms together, slight nod.
