🔬 Research Foundation

CultureBridge AI is built on three pillars of data:

1

NIS Alumni Survey

An empirical questionnaire of Nazarbayev Intellectual School graduates studying in Italy, Hungary, Canada, the USA and other countries. Responses describe real cultural shocks, adaptation strategies and advice for future students.

2

Published Cross-Cultural Models

Six dimensions combine frameworks from Geert Hofstede (Power Distance, Individualism, Uncertainty Avoidance), Edward T. Hall (Context, Time, Proxemics) and Erin Meyer (Expressiveness, direct/indirect feedback).

3

Pragmatic & Politeness Theory

Brown & Levinson's face-threatening acts model underpins the AI Decoder. Each phrase is analysed for its threat to positive face (desire to be liked) and negative face (desire for autonomy).

⚖️ Ethics & Limitations

Anonymity

All alumni survey responses are anonymised. Voices shown on the platform are condensed summaries, not direct quotes, to protect participants' privacy.

Generalisation caveat

Dimension scores describe group tendencies — never every individual. People within any culture vary widely. CultureBridge is a starting point for understanding, not a stereotype machine.

AI transparency

The server-side AI (Gemini / OpenAI) generates cultural interpretations. When the AI is unreachable, a rule-based engine with an expert-curated cache provides answers. The source is always labelled.

Data freshness

Survey data was collected in 2024–2025. Cultural norms evolve. If you notice outdated information, please report it through the admin panel.

🏗️ Technical Architecture

Backend

Python 3.11+, Flask 3.x, SQLAlchemy ORM, Pydantic validation, Gunicorn (production). SQLite for development, PostgreSQL for production.

AI Layer

Server-side integration with Google Gemini and OpenAI APIs. Responses are cached in the database for offline resilience. The rule-based pragma engine guarantees answers even when APIs are down.

Frontend

Vanilla JS (no framework), D3.js for maps, Chart.js for radar, Three.js for 3D elements. CSS custom properties for theming. Progressive enhancement — works without JavaScript for basic content.

Deployment

Docker-ready (Dockerfile + docker-compose). Render.yaml and Procfile for one-click deploy to Render or Railway. CI health checks at /healthz and /api/health.
