📏 Edward T. Hall's Four Distance Zones

💞

Intimate (0–45 cm)

Reserved for partners, close family, and children. Entering this zone with a stranger feels invasive in all cultures.

🤝

Personal (45–120 cm)

Friends and acquaintances. The comfortable range varies: 45 cm in Italy, 110 cm in Canada. Most intercultural misunderstandings happen here.

🏢

Social (120–360 cm)

Formal interactions, strangers, business meetings. Used by default in low-contact cultures like Japan and the UK.

📢

Public (360+ cm)

Public speaking, lectures. No cultural variation — everyone maintains this distance with audiences.
